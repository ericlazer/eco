quick overview

Tender enables businesses worldwide to accept cryptocurrency payments with instant conversion to stablecoins or fiat, featuring low fees and seamless integration.

Built for the real world, Tender bridges the gap between crypto and commerce by providing merchants with easy-to-use payment tools while giving customers the freedom to pay with their preferred cryptocurrencies.

key features

  • One-click merchant onboarding
  • Instant fiat conversion option
  • Multi-currency support
  • QR code payments
  • E-commerce plugins
  • Real-time analytics dashboard

🚀
how to use tender

prerequisites

  • Business registration
  • Bank account (for fiat settlement)
  • Website or physical store
  • KYB verification

getting started

  1. 1

    Merchant Sign-up

    Register your business and complete KYB verification process.

  2. 2

    Integration Setup

    Install plugins for your e-commerce platform or set up API integration.

  3. 3

    Configure Payments

    Choose accepted cryptocurrencies and settlement preferences (crypto or fiat).

  4. 4

    Start Accepting

    Display Tender payment option at checkout or generate QR codes for in-person sales.

  5. 5

    Track & Withdraw

    Monitor transactions via dashboard and withdraw settlements daily.

tips & best practices

  • Offer crypto payment discounts
  • Enable multiple settlement options
  • Use webhooks for order automation
  • Display accepted crypto prominently

Are there monthly fees?

No monthly fees. Pay only 0.9% per successful transaction.
